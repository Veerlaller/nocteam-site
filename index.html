Questions

Straight answers

Do I have to replace my TMS or the software I already use?
No. NocTeam works on top of the tools you already run — your TMS, email, load boards, and accounting. There's nothing to rip out and nothing new for your team to learn.
Is this just another AI chatbot?
No. We use AI agents where they genuinely help, plus plain software and automation everywhere else. The goal is more money in your pocket — not a science project. We start by learning your operation, then build only what actually moves the needle.
How much does it cost?
It depends on what we build and what it's worth to your fleet. We scope it together on the call and start small, so you can see the value before you commit to anything bigger.
I'm not very technical — and neither is my team. Is that a problem?
Not at all. That's the point. We do the building and the setup, white-glove. You keep running your fleet the way you always have — just with less wasted time and money.
How long until I see value?
We start with one high-impact workflow and aim to show results in weeks, not months — not a six-month enterprise rollout. We prove it on something small first, then expand.
How many trucks do you work with?
From owner-operators up to mid-size fleets (roughly 50 trucks). If the big enterprise software priced you out or wasn't built for how you run, you're exactly who we built NocTeam for.
